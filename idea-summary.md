# SponsrMitra: idea summary

## Name

SponsrMitra. "Mitra" is Hindi for friend or ally, which signals trust and being on the student's side.

## Description

SponsrMitra is a verified sponsorship marketplace built specifically for Indian college fest organisers and the brands who want to reach college audiences.

Today, students spend months manually researching companies, guessing who to contact, and getting ignored by most of their outreach, with no idea what a fair ask looks like. Brands, meanwhile, have no single trustworthy channel to discover credible fests worth sponsoring, and rely on scattered cold pitches of uneven quality.

SponsrMitra fixes this with three things generalist event platforms don't offer: verified company contacts who have confirmed sponsorship interest, benchmark data showing what comparable fests actually raised so organisers know what to ask for, and an alumni-introduction feature that lets colleges tap their own alumni at target companies for warm introductions.

**Why they would pay:** colleges may pay a modest subscription for verified listings and benchmark access, saving months of dead-end research. Brands may pay a listing plus success fee, since they get pre-qualified, credible fest leads instead of sifting through unsolicited decks.

## Idea

A verified sponsorship marketplace connecting Indian college fest organisers with companies willing to sponsor, replacing manual cold outreach with verified contacts, benchmark data and alumni-network warm introductions.

**Who is your user?** College fest organising committees (student volunteers running sponsorship outreach) and brand and marketing teams at companies looking to reach college audiences.

**What.** Browse verified companies open to sponsoring fests, see real benchmark data on what similar fests received by category and sponsor type, request a warm introduction through your own college's alumni working at that company, and track where each sponsorship conversation stands.

**Why.** Fest teams currently spend months cold-emailing companies with no idea who to contact, what to ask for, or whether a "confirmed" sponsor will actually follow through. SponsrMitra removes the guesswork and the wasted outreach.

**Use case.** "When I'm building my fest's sponsorship pitch, I want to find companies who've already said yes to sponsoring fests like mine, see what a fair ask looks like, and get a warm intro instead of a cold email, so I stop wasting weeks chasing dead ends."

**Home page.** A greeting header, quick stats (contacts saved, pitches sent, confirmed sponsors) and a recommended-sponsors list. Bottom navigation gives access to Home, Browse, Requests and Profile. In the built app the home page shows the sponsorship target against what is confirmed, likely and hoped for, plus next actions for the team.

## Competitors found

Generalist and regional event sponsorship platforms exist, including Onspon (India), TapSponsor and CampusLink (US), and SponsorMyEvent. Sponbar, a student-built Indian platform, showed no recent public activity when checked. The wedge for SponsrMitra is depth on one narrow job (college fests), verified contacts, fest-specific benchmarks and alumni introductions.
