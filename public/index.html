The problem

Every year, each fest team starts from scratch.

Sponsorship teams change every year. Contacts, past deals and what worked often leave with the people who graduate.

The old way

Cold emails and guesswork

  • A contact list copied from last year, with no way to tell which emails still work.
  • Emails to companies that may not sponsor fests at all.
  • No clear idea of what a fair ask is for a fest of your size.
  • Notes spread across chats and spreadsheets that the next team never sees.
With SponsrMitra

A shared plan for the whole team

  • Your team’s own company list, plus sponsor companies we add as each one is checked.
  • A target for your fest, split into confirmed, likely and hoped for.
  • Introductions through alumni of your own college.
  • One pipeline your whole team works from and hands over to next year’s team.

  • Each range shows how many fests it is based on.

How verification works

A person checks every listing.

Our verification desk checks every fest, brand, alumnus and contact before others can see them, and records how each check was done.

  1. Fests

    Checked through a college email and faculty confirmation, or the student affairs office.

  2. Brands and contacts

    Checked with a test email, a phone call or confirmation from the brand.

  3. Alumni

    Checked against college alumni records or a LinkedIn profile.

  4. Re-checks and reports

    Contacts are re-checked within 90 days. Anyone can report a bounced email or a listing that looks wrong, and the desk reviews it.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Is SponsrMitra open now?

Not yet. We’re setting it up for its first fests and brands. Write to us to ask for early access.

How do I sign in?

Open the app and enter your email. We’ll send you a code to sign in. There are no passwords.

Which fests can join?

Fests at colleges in India. Everything in SponsrMitra assumes India for now. A fest can send requests once our desk has verified it.

How are contact details protected?

A brand contact’s email stays hidden until your fest reaches out to that company. Alumni appear only to fests from their own college. Other fests never see your pipeline.

Where do the benchmark figures come from?

From figures fest teams report to our verification desk. A range appears only when at least five fests are behind it. Until then you see “Not enough data yet”.

Does money move through SponsrMitra?

No. You can record deals and payments to keep track of them, but payments happen outside the app.

How will I hear about replies?

Alerts appear inside the app. There are no email or WhatsApp alerts yet, and the app checks for updates every 20 seconds rather than instantly.

Can I remove my data?

Signed-in users can download or delete their data from their profile page. If you are a company contact and want to be removed, write to us.
